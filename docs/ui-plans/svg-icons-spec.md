# Custom SVG Icons Specification

This document details the visual description, metaphors, and design guidelines for the custom SVG icons to be used on the platform's authentication and onboarding interfaces.

---

## Role Selector Icons

### 1. Farmer Role Icon (`farmer`)
*   **Concept**: Sprout / Wheat Stem / Crop Growth
*   **Visual Metaphor**: Represents agriculture, cultivation, and the primary producer at the start of the value chain.
*   **Detailed Description**:
    *   An organic sprout emerging from the soil.
    *   Features a curved stem rising upwards with two asymmetrical, rounded leaves branching off.
    *   Optionally includes three stylized horizontal lines at the base to represent soil layers.
    *   **Aesthetic**: Friendly, growth-oriented, clean geometric shapes with rounded terminals to avoid looking harsh.
    *   **Current Stand-In**: Lucide `Sprout` / `Wheat`.

### 2. Buyer Role Icon (`buyer`)
*   **Concept**: Shopping Cart / Procurement / Basket
*   **Visual Metaphor**: Represents market transactions, sourcing, trade, and commercial purchasing.
*   **Detailed Description**:
    *   A clean, modern shopping cart profile facing right.
    *   Composed of a angled basket frame with grid lines, a handle bar, and two circular wheels at the base.
    *   Alternatively, a woven market basket with a sturdy top handle, representing local agricultural market sourcing.
    *   **Aesthetic**: Structured, reliable, balanced weight, ensuring lines are bold enough to be legible at small sizes.
    *   **Current Stand-In**: Lucide `ShoppingCart` / `ShoppingBag`.

### 3. Transporter Role Icon (`transporter`)
*   **Concept**: Delivery Truck / Logistics Route
*   **Visual Metaphor**: Represents transportation, supply chain movement, routes, and distribution.
*   **Detailed Description**:
    *   A profile view of a medium-duty delivery truck moving right (representing forward progress).
    *   Features a distinct driver's cab, a large cargo container body, and two wheels.
    *   Can have motion lines behind the container or a tiny leaf decal on the container side to tie back to agriculture.
    *   **Aesthetic**: Dynamic, solid, blocky container silhouette offset by rounded wheels.
    *   **Current Stand-In**: Lucide `Truck` / `Milestone`.

### 4. Warehouse Agent Role Icon (`warehouse_agent`)
*   **Concept**: Warehouse / Storage Depot / Silo
*   **Visual Metaphor**: Represents aggregation, safe keeping, inventory, and storage facilities.
*   **Detailed Description**:
    *   A frontal view of a large warehouse building.
    *   Features a broad, low-pitched gabled roof, an oversized open roll-up bay door in the center, and vertical siding lines.
    *   Could include a small crate icon inside the door to clarify it's a storage facility.
    *   **Aesthetic**: Grounded, symmetrical, structural, communicating security and capacity.
    *   **Current Stand-In**: Lucide `Warehouse`.

## Trust Signal Icons (Left Hero Panel)

### 5. Secure Verification Icon
*   **Concept**: Shield with Checkmark (`ShieldCheck` / `Lock`)
*   **Visual Metaphor**: Represents security, bank-grade verification, and data safety.
*   **Detailed Description**:
    *   A classic medieval shield silhouette with a prominent checkmark positioned in the center.
    *   Smooth curves ending in a sharp bottom point.
    *   **Aesthetic**: Safe, professional, comforting.
    *   **Current Stand-In**: Lucide `ShieldCheck`.

### 6. No Passwords Icon
*   **Concept**: Key or Fingerprint (`KeyRound` / `Fingerprint`)
*   **Visual Metaphor**: Represents ease of access, no credential fatigue, and OTP verification.
*   **Detailed Description**:
    *   A vintage-style key with a large circular bow and geometric teeth.
    *   Oriented at a 45-degree angle.
    *   **Aesthetic**: Modern, simple, recognizable.
    *   **Current Stand-In**: Lucide `KeyRound`.

### 7. Universal Device Support Icon
*   **Concept**: Smartphone or Multi-device (`Smartphone`)
*   **Visual Metaphor**: Responsive, works on simple mobile phones and smartphones.
*   **Detailed Description**:
    *   A vertical smartphone outline with a home button indicator at the bottom and a notch/speaker slot at the top.
    *   Slightly rounded screen corners.
    *   **Aesthetic**: Sleek, minimalistic, mobile-first.
    *   **Current Stand-In**: Lucide `Smartphone`.
